# Apple Bento Grid

## Visual Direction

Apple Bento Grid uses asymmetric rounded modules to divide complex product information into clear, scannable units. Each slide has one dominant story card, several supporting cards, and generous white space. The result should feel modern, ordered, modular, and premium.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#FFFFFF`
- Surface: `#F8F8FA` at 68-76% opacity
- Strong surface: `#F1F1F3`
- Primary text: `#202124`
- Secondary text: `#6E6E73`
- Dark focal tile: `#111113`
- Dark accent: `#303136`
- Mid-gray accent: `#66676C`
- Soft-gray accent: `#96979C`
- Deep-gray accent: `#494A50`
- Cool light-gray surface: `#EDEDF0`

All icons, charts, states, and data markers use grayscale values. Black is reserved for the single most important card on each slide.

## Typography

- Display and body: `Noto Sans SC`
- Latin labels and metrics: `Inter`
- Headlines: 700-900 weight, compact line height, zero letter spacing
- Body: 400-500 weight, muted gray, generous line height
- Small labels: uppercase Inter at 600-700 weight

## Grid

- Canvas: 16:9
- Slide padding: 64px horizontal, 54px top, 76px bottom
- Main layout: 12 columns x 6 rows
- Gap: 16px
- Large card radius: 28px
- Medium card radius: 20px
- Card border: 1px `#DEDEE2`
- Shadows: very soft neutral shadows at 2.8-5% opacity

## Layout Rules

1. Use one dominant card per slide.
2. Mix large, medium, and small spans; avoid a uniform card wall.
3. Keep the dark card below 55% of the slide area.
4. Put metrics, charts, or states in compact supporting cards.
5. Use grayscale contrast, size, and spacing rather than hue to distinguish functions.
6. Do not nest decorative cards inside cards. Small UI mockups are allowed only when they represent actual interface content.
7. Keep all text inside the fixed grid and avoid dense paragraphs.

## Interaction

- Arrow Right, Space, or Page Down advances.
- Arrow Left or Page Up goes back.
- Home and End jump to the first and last slide.
- Touch swipe is supported.
- Page number and progress bar persist outside the slide content.

## Do

- Keep the canvas white or very light gray.
- Use black as the visual anchor.
- Use small grayscale chips to communicate function.
- Let card size communicate information hierarchy.
- Use generous spacing and short copy.

## Do Not

- Do not add gradients as page backgrounds.
- Do not introduce any chromatic accent colors.
- Do not make every card the same size.
- Do not add heavy borders or strong drop shadows.
- Do not add decorative blobs or floating ornaments.
